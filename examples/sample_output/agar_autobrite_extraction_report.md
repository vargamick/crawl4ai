### Product Description

**What is Autobrite?**
AUTOBRITE is a unique floor cleaner and restorer which cleans effectively and simultaneously replenishes the floor surface with acrylic polish. It is designed for damp-mopping and autoscrubbing, and will leave the floor clean and ready for buffing to a very high finish. Alternatively, it may be used as a quick acting spray-buff solution.

### Key Benefits

1. AUTOBRITE is a convenient, two-in-one product that polishes and cleans in one operation
2. If used regularly, AUTOBRITE will create a beautiful, glossy film of floor polish

### How It Works

AUTOBRITE removes all types of routine floor soilage through a two-stage cleaning mechanism:

1. **Attack of soilage**: Detergent compounds emulsify and remove dirt from the floor
2. **Preferential adherence**: Polish components form a protective film when the floor dries

### Usage Applications

**For Use On:**
- Vinyl floors
- Timber floors  
- Terrazzo floors
- Quarry tile floors
- Ceramic-tile floors

**Optimal Usage:**
- Ideal for slow to medium speed buffing
- NOT intended for ultra-high speed buffing (greater than 1200 rpm)
